About this site

Source

The lectures, their audio and the Persian transcripts come from the official site of Hujjat al-Islam Aminikhah, aminikhaah.ir, and each session page links back to its source page. They remain his and his site's work and are shown here, with credit, to make the series easier to study. If the speaker or his site would like anything changed or removed, please open an issue on this site's GitHub page.

The transcripts

The Persian transcripts are the source site's automatic transcriptions and are given as published. They contain mishearings and gaps.

The summaries

Each session has an English summary prepared with AI assistance and reviewed against the Persian transcript. A summary is not a translation: it follows the lecture, keeps the speaker's own claims as his, and gives Qurʾān verses in the Sahih International translation.

Names and terms

Arabic and Persian names and terms are transliterated.
