The speaker

Hujjat al-Islam Mustafa Aminikhah

Mustafa Aminikhah is a Twelver Shiʿi cleric who lectures mainly to engineering and science students and publishes his talks on his own website. The facts at the left come from public profiles of him; the rest of this page draws on the lectures themselves.

What the lectures show

  • He speaks to engineering and science students in a deliberately informal style, with jokes and everyday examples, and says he adopted it to hold the attention of students who would not otherwise listen.
  • He often cites ʿAllāma Ṭabāṭabāʾī's al-Mīzān and Mullā Ṣadrā's Asfār, and a chain of teachers in the mystical line of Mullā Ḥusayn-Qulī Hamadānī, Qāḍī and Pahlavān Tihrānī.
  • The audio of his talks is published at aminikhaah.ir, and each session page here links to it. The Persian transcripts shown on this site come from there.
  • In the question sessions he says that the series grew far beyond the student circle he meant it for, reached Persian speakers around the world and was translated into Arabic.

How he reads the book

He treats the three accounts as testimony whose truth he cannot prove, but whose content matches the Qurʾān and narrations. His main points: that the human being has a "homeland" beyond matter; that deeds take form in the barzakh; that wronging other people (ḥaqq al-nās) is the heaviest burden; that certainty (yaqīn) is what the experiences are for; and that paradise itself is not the goal. The summaries report his claims as his own.
