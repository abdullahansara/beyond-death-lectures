Notes and credits

Source and rights

The lectures, their audio and the Persian transcripts come from the official site of Hujjat al-Islam Aminikhah, aminikhaah.ir (each session page links to its source page). They remain his and his site's. They are reproduced here, with credit, for study and research. If the rights-holder or the speaker asks for anything to be changed or removed, please open an issue on this site's GitHub page and it will be done promptly.

The Persian transcripts

The transcripts are the source site's automatic transcriptions, which say so themselves. They contain mishearings, missing words and misplaced sentences, and square brackets in them are the transcriber's. They are given unchanged.

The English summaries

  • Written with AI assistance, session by session, after reading each Persian transcript in full. They are summaries and not translations.
  • Qurʾān verses are given in the Sahih International translation, with sūra and verse. Where the speaker's citation was wrong or garbled, the verse is identified and the correction noted.
  • Hadith, stories about scholars and political remarks are the speaker's statements. Most narrations could not be traced and are marked so; none of them has been verified here.
  • Each summary ends with "Flags on the transcript text", listing garbled or doubtful passages.
  • The summaries of sessions 1-4 are the earliest and use Persian script for some names and verses; sessions 5-41 are in transliteration only.

Audio

Where a session has audio on this site, it is a compressed copy of the original recording; otherwise the session page links to the original.

Transliteration

Arabic and Persian names and terms are transliterated in the style of the International Journal of Middle East Studies, without diacritics for the Persian pronunciation of common words.
